# ESX: Syndicate Roleplay

The Official Roleplay server of ESX, where stories are played, and legends are made.

## Why Syndicate RP?
Built by the core ESX Team, Syndicate delivers a polished, immersive roleplay experience. With top-tier performance, premium assets, and a vibrant community, this isn’t just a server—it’s a living, breathing city that redefines FiveM roleplay.

### Dive into a world designed for depth and excitement:

- **Optimized Framework**: A Custom version of our imfamous ESX: Legacy framework, tailored by our world-class team.
- **Unique Style**: Drive custom cars and wear exclusive outfits, from Sinners Mod Shop’s British-inspired designs to lore-friendly emergency vehicles and Gabz creations.
- **Exclusive Scripts**: Powered by creators like Wasabi, Quasar, LB Scripts, Envi-Scripts and RCore for unmatched variety.
- **Living World**: Realistic progression, balanced economy, and intricate systems bring the city to life.
- **ToS Compliant**: Built for stability, fairness, and longevity.

### A Team That Cares
Our dedicated developers, designers, and staff work tirelessly to keep the city dynamic, fair, and full of possibilities. We’re not just building a server—we’re shaping the future of roleplay.

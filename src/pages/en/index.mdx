# Welcome to ESX: Legacy
The premier framework for crafting immersive and endlessly customizable FiveM servers

## What is ESX?
Started in 2017, ESX is a passionate, non-profit orgnaisation that empowers the FiveM community with free, open-source tools.
Our cornerstone project, <strong>ESX: Legacy</strong>, is a dynamic framework designed to make building captivating roleplay servers effortless and inspiring.
With its intuitive design and boundless flexibility, Legacy equips you to create unforgettable experiences.
<br/>

We are:
- **Open Source**:  Freely accessible and collaboratively built, ESX invites everyone to innovate.
- **Community Driven**: Crafted by creators, for creators.
- **Feature Rich**: Loaded with all the tools needed to bring your boldest server ideas to life.
